Copyright Policy

How we handle copyright infringement, takedown notices and repeat infringers.

Last updated January 2024

LUDINO LIVE respects the intellectual property rights of others and expects every user to do the same.

Copyright infringement

Our platform rules do not allow livestreaming or uploading content that infringes another party’s copyrights. You are responsible for making sure you own—or have permission or another legally valid basis to use—the content you share.

Not every unauthorized use amounts to infringement. Copyright laws may recognize fair use, fair dealing, or other jurisdiction-specific exceptions. This policy is not legal advice; seek independent legal counsel if you are unsure about the rights to content you plan to use.

Our role as a platform provider

As a U.S.-incorporated service provider, LUDINO LIVE follows the safe-harbor procedures in the U.S. Digital Millennium Copyright Act, 17 U.S.C. § 512, and respects other applicable copyright laws. We respond to valid notices and may remove or disable access to infringing material.

Enforcement

When we determine that a user has infringed copyright, we may remove content, suspend or terminate the account, or refuse new registrations. We may treat a user found to have infringed copyright two or more times as a repeat infringer.

Submitting a takedown notice

A copyright owner or authorized representative should provide:

  1. Legal name and complete contact information.
  2. A description of the copyrighted work and the copyright owner.
  3. A precise description or URL showing where the disputed content appears.
  4. A good-faith statement that the use is not authorized by the owner, its agent, or the law.
  5. A request to remove or disable access to the material.
  6. A statement, under penalty of perjury, that the notice is accurate and you are authorized to act.
  7. Your physical or electronic signature.

After receiving a valid notice, we may remove or disable access to the material and notify the affected user in accordance with applicable law.
