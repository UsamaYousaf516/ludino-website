You can submit a request here, or use Settings → Delete Account inside the LUDINO app.

Enter your User IDLocate the 8-digit User ID shown on your LUDINO profile and enter it in the request form.

Data retention after deletion

Most account information is removed through the deletion process. Certain records may be retained where needed for verification, security, legal, or regulatory purposes.

01

02

03

Transaction history

In-app purchases and payment records required by financial regulations.

04

Relevant personal details if you registered as an agency or talent.
